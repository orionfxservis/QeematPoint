Privacy & Data Control

User Data Deletion

StopBuy.pk respects your privacy and gives you control over your personal information. You may request deletion of your StopBuy account and associated personal data at any time.

Requesting Data Deletion

If you have used Google, Facebook, or another supported authentication provider to access StopBuy.pk, you can request deletion of your StopBuy account and the personal information associated with that account.

How to Request Deletion

Follow these steps to request deletion of your StopBuy account.

1

Send a deletion request

Send an email requesting deletion of your StopBuy account and associated personal information.

2

Provide your account information

Include the email address associated with your StopBuy account. If you registered using Google or Facebook, please use the same email address associated with that login.

3

Account verification

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the deletion request belongs to the account owner.

4

Data deletion

After verification, we will process the request and delete the applicable account information, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate retention requirements.

Submit Your Request

Send your deletion request to our support team.

What Data May Be Deleted?

Account Information

Account profile and authentication information associated with your StopBuy account.

Personal Data

Personal information stored in connection with your StopBuy account, where applicable.

Login Connection

The association between your StopBuy account and supported social login providers may be removed.

Retained Information

Certain information may be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate business requirements.

Google & Facebook Login

If you signed in to StopBuy using Google or Facebook, removing StopBuy's access through your Google or Facebook account does not necessarily delete your StopBuy account. To request deletion of the StopBuy account and associated information, please submit a deletion request using the email address associated with your StopBuy account.

For more information about how StopBuy handles personal data, please review our Privacy Policy.
